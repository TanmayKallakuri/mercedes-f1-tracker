When the question is about one venue:

1. Call get_mercedes_results with the round number for that venue (or the full season and find it by circuit name).
2. Report both drivers' grid and finishing positions, points, and any fastest lap.
3. Compare the team's haul to its best and worst rounds of the season from get_standings context if the visitor asks "where was Mercedes strongest".
4. Do not speculate about car setup, tyre strategy or telemetry; the data covers results only.
